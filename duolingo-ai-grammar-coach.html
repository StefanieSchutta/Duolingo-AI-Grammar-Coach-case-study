It motivates.

05 · Solution

AI Grammar Coach

A Premium feature that explains errors, detects patterns, and builds a personalized learning path.

Feature 01

Explain My Mistake

Every error triggers an instant AI explanation — the grammatical rule, in plain language, right when it matters most.

Lesson 312/20
Fill in the blank:
"I __ to London last year."
Before · Standard
✗ Wrong
"go" → Correct: went
After · AI Coach
✦ AI Coach
✗ You wrote: "go"
You used the present tense. "Last year" describes a completed event — Simple Past is required.
✓ Correct
"I went to London last year."

Grammar Timeline

Abstract tense logic becomes visual — a spatial axis that makes the difference between Simple Past and Present Perfect immediately clear.
